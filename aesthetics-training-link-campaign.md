## Verification basis (read first)

All course variations and destination URLs were checked against the live aesthetictraining.ca site before use.

**Verified program pages (destinations):**

| Program (as published) | URL | Length | Eligibility / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laser Technician (Medical Laser Certification) | `/courses/laser-technician` | 4 weeks | Requires licensed esthetician/healthcare credential + CPR. Curriculum includes **laser hair removal** + photofacials. |
| Medical Aesthetician | `/courses/medical-aesthetician` | 8 weeks | Facials, peels, dermaplaning, microdermabrasion, LED/ultrasound. **No laser, no microneedling.** |
| Botox & Filler (Injectable Aesthetics) | `/courses/botox-filler` | 5 days | **Healthcare providers only** (nurse / MD / IMD / medical-director authorization). |

| Aesthetic Diploma (Diploma in Medical Aesthetics) | `/courses/aesthetic-diploma` | 16 weeks | Bundles foundations + laser + injectables + **PRP** + business. |

**Not used:** `/courses/prp` returns **HTTP 404** — there is no standalone PRP page. The single "PRP" phrase (#50) is routed to the **Aesthetic Diploma**, which is the only verified program that includes PRP.

**Compliance rules applied to every anchor and article:**
- Completion certificates are described as **certificates of course completion from an independent provider** — never as a government licence or independent accreditation.
- The provider has **one Markham campus**. Only Markham uses direct "in Markham" phrasing. All other cities use **"near," "serving," "for residents of," or "across"** — never implying a campus in those cities.
- Botox/filler anchors and copy state the **medical-professionals-only** eligibility.
- No claims of search volume, competition level, or guaranteed ranking. These are **candidate search phrases**, not verified volume data.
