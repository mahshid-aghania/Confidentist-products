FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this mock exam designed for?
The AFK Mock Exam series is designed for internationally trained dentists preparing for the AFK (Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge) examination — one of the key steps toward obtaining a dental license in Canada.
Can I purchase a single exam instead of a package?
Yes. You can enroll in any individual full or mini mock exam at $400 CAD per session. Packages offer savings when purchasing multiple exams together.
What happens if I miss a discussion session?
No recordings will be provided for missed live discussion sessions. It is essential to attend all scheduled sessions in real time. If you cannot attend, please contact us in advance to explore options.
Is the in-person option available for all exams?
The in-person option with live discussions is only available for Mock Exam 3 (January 8, 2027). All other exams are conducted online only.
How do I enroll and what is the payment process?
Click any "Enroll Now" or "Select Package" button to go to the enrollment page. Payment is processed securely through the ConfiDentist website. Once enrolled, you will receive confirmation and access details by email.
When will I receive exam details after enrolling?
After enrollment, you will receive a confirmation email with all session details, including access links, start times, and preparation guidelines, at least one week before your scheduled exam date.
