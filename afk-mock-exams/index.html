FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this mock exam designed for?
The AFK Mock Exam series is designed for internationally trained dentists preparing for the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) examination — a key step toward obtaining a dental license in Canada.
Can I purchase a single exam instead of a package?
Yes. Use the Bundle Builder above to select any individual full mock exam at $500 CAD per session. Bundle pricing drops the per-exam cost as you add more: 2 mocks for $900, 3 for $1,200, and all 4 for $1,500. The mini mocks are the one exception — they are sold only as a complete set of 4 and cannot be purchased individually.
How much are the mini mock exams?
The 4 mini mocks are sold together as a single package for $400 CAD. If you enroll in all 4 full mock exams, the mini mock package is discounted to $200 CAD — bringing the complete 8-exam bundle to $1,700 CAD.
What happens if I miss a discussion session?
No recordings are provided for missed live discussion sessions. It is essential to attend all scheduled sessions in real time. Please contact us in advance if you have a conflict.
Is the in-person option available for all exams?
The in-person option with live discussions is only available for Mock Exam 3 (January 8, 2027). All other exams are conducted online.
How do I enroll and pay?
Click any enroll button or use the Bundle Builder to select your exams. Payment is processed securely through the ConfiDentist website. You will receive a confirmation email with all session details.
When will I receive session details after enrolling?
You will receive a confirmation email with access links, start times, and preparation guidelines at least one week before your first scheduled exam date.
