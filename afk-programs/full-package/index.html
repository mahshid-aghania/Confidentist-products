Which Package Is Right for You?

Choose Extended if…

You want a longer preparation period, have a significant study gap, work while studying or prefer a more personalized study plan.

Choose Comprehensive if…

You can maintain a more intensive study schedule — whether you're a first-time candidate or a retaker who wants complete preparation in a shorter period.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the course online?
Yes. The AFK Full Package is delivered primarily online, combining live Zoom classes with pre-recorded lectures accessed through your ConfiPortal account. Occasional in-person sessions may be available depending on the cohort.
Is mentorship included?
Yes. Private mentorship is included in both the Extended and Comprehensive packages, along with membership in the VIP WhatsApp group. The Extended package additionally includes a personalized study plan and a mid-course progress evaluation session.
Are booklets delivered outside Canada?
Both packages include 11 updated booklets covering 13 topics. For questions about delivery to your location, please contact us at info@confidentist.ca before registering so we can confirm the available options.
When will I receive course access?
Access is generally provided within 2–3 business days after registration. You'll receive your ConfiPortal account details and joining information by email.
Are installment plans available?
An installment payment option is available for the Comprehensive package. For details on installment arrangements, please reach out to info@confidentist.ca.
Are returning-student discounts available?
If you are a returning ConfiDentist student, contact info@confidentist.ca to ask about available options before you register.
Is live attendance mandatory?
The program blends live Zoom sessions with pre-recorded lectures you can watch on demand through ConfiPortal. If you cannot attend a live session, please contact us so we can advise on the best approach for your cohort.
What should I do if I experience technical issues?
For technical support, contact admin@confidentist.ca, info@confidentist.ca or confiportal@confidentist.ca. You can also call +1 437 370 0122.
