Two Pathways

Two Flexible Ways to Prepare for the AFK Exam

Every AFK candidate has different needs. Some students want a complete preparation system with structured guidance from beginning to end. Others already have AFK experience and only need help strengthening specific subjects. Explore both options below to find the program that best matches your current stage.

Complete Preparation

AFK Full Package

A complete and structured AFK preparation pathway for candidates who want comprehensive subject coverage, organized study support and guidance throughout their exam preparation.

Best suited for

  • First-time AFK candidates
  • Students who want a complete study structure
  • Candidates returning after a long study gap
  • Students who need broader subject coverage
  • Candidates who value mentorship, reviews and mock-exam preparation

A comprehensive, structured pathway with broad AFK subject coverage and guided study support. Full details are available on the program page.

Explore the Full Package Flexible Preparation

AFK Subject-Based Program

A flexible preparation option that allows candidates to select individual AFK subjects and focus their time and budget on the areas where they need the most support.

Best suited for

  • Previous AFK students and retakers
  • Candidates with specific weak subjects
  • Dentists with limited study time
  • Students seeking focused revision
  • Candidates who want to combine selected subjects

Choose one subject, combine several subjects, or create a customized AFK pathway built around exactly what you need.
