Is Bundling Right for You?

What You Gain by Combining Programs

A lower total price

Bundled packages cost less than buying the same programs one at a time — the AFK mock bundle alone saves $500 against individual booking.

One continuous plan

Your programs are sequenced against your exam dates from the start, rather than stitched together as each one becomes urgent.

No repeated onboarding

The same instructors and mentors follow you across each stage, so you are not re-explaining your background every time you begin something new.

Predictable payments

Interest-free installment plans spread the cost of a bundle across the length of your preparation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine any two programs into a bundle?
Published bundles are listed on this page. For other combinations, contact an advisor — custom packages are arranged individually around the exams you are preparing for.
Are payment plans available on bundles?
Yes. ConfiDentist offers various interest-free payment plans, including on the Brilliant Bundle. For details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
Do bundle components have to be taken at the same time?
No. A bundle groups the programs and the price; the components are scheduled across your preparation timeline. An advisor will map them to your exam dates.
Is the bundle price the same as buying each program separately?
No — bundling is the lower-cost route. Each bundle above shows the combined price, and where a direct comparison exists the saving is stated alongside it.
Can I add private sessions to a bundle?
Yes. One-on-one private sessions can be added to any package. Ask an advisor when your bundle is being arranged.
