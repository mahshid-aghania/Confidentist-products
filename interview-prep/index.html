How It Works

From First Session to Interview Day

Every candidate arrives with different strengths, so the programme starts by finding yours before it starts rehearsing answers.

Assessment

An initial conversation to understand which programs you are applying to, the interview format they use, and where you feel least confident.

Practice

Mock interviews run under realistic conditions — timed stations, unfamiliar prompts, and an interviewer who does not go easy on you.

Feedback

Specific, written feedback on content, structure and delivery, so you know exactly what to change before the next session.

Refinement

Repeat practice on the scenarios that gave you trouble, until your answers are consistent under pressure rather than only on a good day.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this program for?
It is designed for internationally trained dentists preparing for admissions interviews at Canadian dental programs. If you are unsure whether it fits the program you are applying to, contact an advisor and describe your situation.
Are the sessions online?
Yes. Sessions are delivered live via Zoom, so you can take part from anywhere — and practise in the same virtual format many programs now use for interviews.
How many sessions will I need?
That depends on your interview format, your timeline and how much practice you have already had. An advisor will recommend a plan after an initial conversation.
Do you cover MMI as well as panel interviews?
Yes. Practice covers both timed Multiple Mini Interview stations and traditional panel-style interviews, so you are ready for whichever format your program uses.
Are payment plans available?
Yes. ConfiDentist offers various interest-free payment plans. For details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
What should I do if I experience technical issues?
For technical support, contact confiportal@confidentist.ca or admin@confidentist.ca. You can also call +1 437 370 0122.
