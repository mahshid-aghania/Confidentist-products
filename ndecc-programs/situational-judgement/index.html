FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the course online or in person?
It's a blended, private in-person course. The first month is delivered as recorded videos through ConfiPortal, and the following two months are in-person classes in small groups of up to 7 people.
What's the difference between Core and Comprehensive?
Both include the full course — 64 hours of sessions, 200+ scenarios, personalized feedback and resources. The Comprehensive package additionally includes an intensive program of 8 full mock exams with one-on-one feedback and private post-mock discussions, structured for completion in 4 weeks.
Can I take the mock exams without the course?
Yes. The Situational Judgement Mock Exam program is available separately (2–8 mocks) from $1,200 CAD + HST.
Are installment plans available?
Yes. Both packages offer an interest-free installment option (2 + 1 payments). For details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
Is live attendance mandatory?
Live attendance is highly recommended for the in-person sessions, given the interactive, role-play nature of the course. If you have scheduling concerns, contact us to discuss your cohort.
Who can I contact with questions?
Reach out to info@confidentist.ca or call +1 437 370 0122. For technical support, contact admin@confidentist.ca or confiportal@confidentist.ca.
