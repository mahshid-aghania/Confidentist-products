What Private Sessions Can Cover

Any part of the ConfiDentist curriculum can be taught one-on-one — most often alongside a course, to reinforce the parts that need more time.

ACJ

Is This Right for You?

Private Sessions Make the Most Sense If…

  • You are retaking an exam and already know which areas cost you the marks.
  • You are strong across most of the syllabus but one subject is holding you back.
  • Your work or family schedule does not fit a fixed course timetable.
  • You are already enrolled in a course and want extra time on the hardest material.
  • You prefer asking questions directly rather than in a group setting.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are private sessions online or in person?
Sessions are normally delivered live online via Zoom, so you can join from anywhere. Where a subject requires hands-on work, ask an advisor what in-person options are available.
How many hours should I book?
That depends on the subject and how much ground you need to cover. An advisor will suggest a number of hours after discussing where you are and when your exam is.
Can I add private sessions to a course I am already taking?
Yes. Private sessions are frequently added alongside a course or a bundle to spend more time on the most difficult material.
Can I choose my instructor?
Instructors are matched to the subject you need. If you have worked with a particular instructor before and would like to continue, mention it when you contact an advisor.
Are payment plans available?
Yes. ConfiDentist offers various interest-free payment plans. For details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
What should I do if I experience technical issues?
For technical support, contact confiportal@confidentist.ca or admin@confidentist.ca. You can also call +1 437 370 0122.
