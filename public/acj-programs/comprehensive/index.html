Frequently Asked Questions

Is the course delivered entirely online?
Yes. The ACJ Comprehensive program is delivered online through live Zoom lectures and pre-recorded content accessible via our portal. Occasional in-person sessions may be available at our North York office.
Is mentorship included?
Yes — a dedicated mentor supports you throughout the course, alongside membership in the exclusive VIP WhatsApp group.
When do I get access after registering?
Access is granted within 2–3 business days of registration.
Are installment plans available?
Yes — interest-free installment plans are available. Email info@confidentist.ca or call +1 437 370 0122 to set one up.
Can I pause the course?
Yes — the course can be paused and resumed with help from our team.
Is live attendance mandatory?
No, but live attendance is highly recommended for the best results, since sessions include live discussion.
