Overview

What Is the Assessment of Clinical Judgement?

The Assessment of Clinical Judgement (ACJ) is one of the examinations administered by the National Dental Examination Board of Canada (NDEB) as part of the equivalency process for internationally trained dentists. It is designed to assess clinical judgement and decision-making across a range of dental disciplines.

ConfiDentist's ACJ Comprehensive program helps you prepare through structured lectures, quizzes with live discussion, a radiology and case-based practice series, and a full sequence of mini and full mock examinations — supported by private mentorship throughout.

Exam content, eligibility and dates are set by the NDEB and may change. Always confirm the current requirements and schedule directly with the NDEB before you apply.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ACJ course online?
The ACJ Comprehensive program is delivered primarily online with live and pre-recorded sessions. An in-office X-ray practice library is also part of the program.
How long is the program?
The ACJ Comprehensive program runs for 6 months and is offered in cohorts aligned to upcoming NDEB exam dates.
When will I receive access after registering?
Course access is generally provided within 2–3 business days after registration. If you have not received your access details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
What's included in the program?
The program includes 111 total sessions, 20 quizzes with live discussions, an X-ray practice library, 10 mini-mock exams and 4 full mock exams with discussion sessions, private mentorship and VIP WhatsApp group membership. See the program details page for the full list.
Are installment plans available?
For information about payment and installment options, please contact info@confidentist.ca before registering.
Which cohort should I choose?
Choose the cohort aligned to the NDEB exam date you plan to sit. If you're unsure, speak with an advisor and we'll help you decide.
Can I buy just the booklets?
Yes. ConfiDentist's ACJ Booklets are available separately as a study resource. Pricing is shown on the product page.
Who should I contact for technical support?
For technical support, contact admin@confidentist.ca, info@confidentist.ca or confiportal@confidentist.ca. You can also call +1 437 370 0122.
