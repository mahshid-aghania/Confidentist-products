About ACJ

What Is the Assessment of Clinical Judgement?

The Assessment of Clinical Judgement (ACJ) is one of the examinations administered by the National Dental Examination Board of Canada (NDEB) as part of the equivalency process for internationally trained dentists. It assesses clinical judgement and decision-making across a range of dental disciplines.

ConfiDentist's ACJ program prepares you with live sessions, quizzes, a full mock-exam sequence and private mentorship — everything you need to approach the exam with confidence.

Exam content, eligibility and dates are set by the NDEB and may change. Always confirm the current requirements and schedule directly with the NDEB before you apply.
