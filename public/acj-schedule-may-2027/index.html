How the Program Runs

Your Path to the May 2027 Exam

The program is built in four phases — a Foundation phase to establish core knowledge, a Live Learning phase working through the full syllabus with quizzes and discussions, a Mini-Mock phase to build exam stamina, and a Full Mock phase of timed practice exams with detailed review before your exam sitting.

Please be advised that the information below is subject to change. Session dates, times and formats may be adjusted; registered students are notified of any updates through ConfiPortal and the VIP WhatsApp group.
