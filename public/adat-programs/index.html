Overview

What Is the Advanced Dental Admission Test?

The Advanced Dental Admission Test, or ADAT, is a standardized examination developed by the American Dental Association to help advanced dental education programs evaluate applicants for postgraduate training. It measures biomedical knowledge, clinical understanding, research interpretation and evidence-based decision-making.

ADAT scores may be considered alongside:

  • Academic performance
  • Clinical experience
  • Research experience
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Personal statements
  • Other program-specific admission requirements

ADAT requirements vary between institutions, and not every postgraduate dental program requires it. Please confirm the requirements of each university or specialty program directly before you apply.

Exam Coverage

Core Areas of the ADAT

The ADAT consists of 200 multiple-choice questions divided across three main areas.

Biomedical Sciences

Foundational biomedical knowledge relevant to advanced dental education and clinical practice.

Clinical Sciences

Application of clinical concepts across major areas of dentistry.

Research & Evidence-Based Dentistry

Research interpretation, critical thinking and evidence-based clinical decision-making.

Exam structure is set by the American Dental Association. Confirm current details on the official ADAT website.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the ADAT courses online?
Yes. Both ADAT programs use a hybrid learning format delivered online, so you can prepare from anywhere.
Are sessions live or pre-recorded?
The programs combine live sessions with pre-recorded lectures. Live sessions are scheduled, while pre-recorded content can be watched on demand through your ConfiPortal account.
When will I receive access after registering?
Course access is provided after registration and confirmation. If you have not received your access details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
How long will course materials remain available?
For both the Comprehensive and Crash 2026 programs, course-material access is available until the end of August 2027.
Are installment plans available?
For information about payment and installment options, please contact info@confidentist.ca before registering.
Are booklets shipped outside Canada?
The programs include comprehensive booklets. For questions about delivery to your location, contact info@confidentist.ca so we can confirm the available options.
Is attendance at live sessions mandatory?
The programs blend live sessions with pre-recorded lectures you can access on demand. If you cannot attend a live session, please contact us so we can advise on the best approach.
Can I request a tailored ADAT course?
Yes. If you need a specialized plan, ConfiDentist can assess your goals, schedule and preferred learning approach to discuss a tailored option. Reach out via our contact page.
Who should I contact for technical support?
For technical support, contact admin@confidentist.ca, info@confidentist.ca or confiportal@confidentist.ca. You can also call +1 437 370 0122.
