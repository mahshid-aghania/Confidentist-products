Adding Microneedling to Your Service Menu

Microneedling & Collagen Induction5 min read

For an existing practitioner, microneedling is a popular menu addition — a well-known treatment with broad appeal and a short path to competence for those who already meet the prerequisites.

A natural add-on

It pairs well with skin-focused practices and existing facials. Someone pursuing a microneedling certification for Mississauga residents is usually an esthetician or clinician widening what they can offer.

Offer it well

Invest in proper training and equipment, set honest expectations, and only treat within your competence. Confirm prerequisites before enrolling.
