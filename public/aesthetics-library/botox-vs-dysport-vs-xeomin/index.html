Botox vs Dysport vs Xeomin: What Practitioners Learn

Injectables (Medical Professionals)5 min read

Botox, Dysport, and Xeomin are all neurotoxins used to soften dynamic lines, but they differ in formulation, dosing, and onset. Trained injectors learn these distinctions to treat predictably.

Why the differences matter

Dosing and diffusion are not interchangeable between products, so understanding each is a safety matter. A Botox and filler training for Toronto clinicians covers product selection alongside technique and aftercare.
