Building a Laser Service Menu

Laser Technician Training5 min read

Once trained, you or your clinic will decide which laser services to offer. Building a menu thoughtfully — starting with what you do well and expanding as competence grows — beats offering everything at once.

Start focused

Hair reduction is a common anchor service; rejuvenation and other treatments can follow. Practitioners who completed a laser technician training option for Vaughan residents often add services gradually as they gain reps and confidence.

Match menu to skill

Only offer what you are trained and competent to deliver safely. Expanding responsibly protects clients and your reputation.
