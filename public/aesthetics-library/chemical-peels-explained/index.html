Chemical Peels Explained: Depths and Who Performs Them

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

Chemical peels range from light, superficial exfoliation to deeper resurfacing, and the depth determines who can perform them and in what setting. Understanding that spectrum is core to skin-treatment training.

Depth drives everything

Superficial peels are common in medical-aesthetics practice; deeper peels carry more risk and more restriction. A medical aesthetics training in Markham program covers peel selection, skin prep, and aftercare so treatments stay safe.

Match to skin and goals

Good training teaches you to match peel depth to skin type and client goals — and to recognise when to refer.
