Combining Microneedling With Other Treatments

Microneedling & Collagen Induction5 min read

Microneedling is often combined with complementary treatments — serums, certain facials, or other modalities — to enhance results. Sensible combination requires knowing what pairs safely and what does not.

Combinations with care

Timing and product choice matter, and some combinations are best spaced apart. Practitioners exploring microneedling training serving the GTA learn which pairings are appropriate and how to sequence them.

Safety leads

Only combine treatments you are trained to deliver, and keep the client’s skin tolerance front of mind.
