Dermal Filler Types and Brands: A Trainee&rsquo;s Overview

Injectables (Medical Professionals)5 min read

Dermal fillers come in several categories — most commonly hyaluronic-acid based — with different properties suited to different areas and goals. Knowing the material is as important as knowing the technique.

Material shapes the plan

Thicker products suit deeper structural work; softer ones suit fine areas. A dermal filler course near Richmond Hill covers how product choice, injection site, and anatomy fit together.

For eligible clinicians

Filler training is restricted to qualified healthcare providers. Always work within your scope and the current rules.
