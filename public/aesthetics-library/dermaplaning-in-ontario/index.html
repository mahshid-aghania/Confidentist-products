What Is Dermaplaning, and Who Can Perform It?

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

Dermaplaning uses a sterile blade to remove dead surface skin and fine vellus hair, leaving a smoother complexion and better product absorption. It is a popular, relatively gentle treatment.

Where it fits

Dermaplaning is commonly part of a medical aesthetician’s menu. Someone taking a medical aesthetics course for Richmond Hill residents will usually learn it alongside facials and peels as part of a skin-treatment toolkit.

Technique and safety

Proper technique and sanitation matter. Confirm the current rules for who may perform it with the relevant Ontario authorities.
