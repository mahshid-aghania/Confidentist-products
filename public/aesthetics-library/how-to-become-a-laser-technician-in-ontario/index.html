How to Become a Laser Technician in Ontario

Laser Technician Training5 min read

Laser treatments are a core service in modern aesthetics clinics, and the technicians who operate the devices are in steady demand. Becoming one means pairing a technical understanding of how light interacts with skin and hair with careful, supervised clinical practice.

The realistic path

Most people enter through a focused certification after already holding an esthetician or healthcare credential. The laser technician course in Markham offered by Ziba is built on that foundation, so training concentrates on laser physics, skin typing, safety, and hands-on technique rather than starting from zero.

What to confirm first

Check the prerequisites (typically an esthetician or healthcare credential plus current CPR), the amount of supervised device time, and the credential awarded. Completing a course earns a certificate of completion — valuable to employers, but not a government licence, and no course guarantees a job on its own.
