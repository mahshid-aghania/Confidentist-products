Laser Skin Rejuvenation and Photofacials

Laser Technician Training5 min read

Beyond hair removal, lasers and intense light are used for skin rejuvenation — evening tone, reducing the look of sun damage, and stimulating a fresher complexion. These "photofacial" treatments share the same safety foundations as hair reduction.

One skill set, several applications

A technician who understands wavelengths and skin typing can offer a wider menu. People exploring laser hair removal training serving the GTA usually find the rejuvenation modules just as valuable, since the underlying safety principles carry across treatments.

Set expectations honestly

Rejuvenation results build gradually over sessions. Training should cover consultation and realistic outcomes as much as device operation.
