Laser Treatment Aftercare and Client Care

Laser Technician Training5 min read

A laser treatment does not end when the device switches off. Clear aftercare — sun protection, soothing, and what to expect over the following days — protects results and builds trust.

Part of the skill set

Technicians who communicate aftercare well see better outcomes and fewer problems. Programs delivered laser technician training across the Greater Toronto Area should treat consultation and aftercare as core content, not an afterthought.

Documentation

Recording settings and advice given is good practice and good protection. Expect training to cover it.
