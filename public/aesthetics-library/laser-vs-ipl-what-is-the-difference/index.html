Laser vs IPL: What&rsquo;s the Difference?

Laser Technician Training5 min read

"Laser" and "IPL" are often used interchangeably, but they are different technologies. A laser emits a single, focused wavelength; IPL (intense pulsed light) emits a broad spectrum. Each suits different treatments.

Why trainees learn both

Clinics use both, so understanding the distinction helps a technician choose the right tool and set expectations. Anyone comparing laser training for Mississauga residents should check that the curriculum explains these differences rather than treating all devices as one.

Safety applies to both

Different technology, same discipline: skin typing, test spots, and documented settings protect clients regardless of the device.
