LED Light Therapy in a Modern Service Menu

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

LED light therapy uses specific wavelengths to support skin — commonly to calm, to target blemish-causing bacteria, or to encourage a healthier-looking complexion. It is gentle and often paired with other treatments.

A useful add-on

LED is low-risk and complements facials and post-treatment recovery, which makes it a practical menu addition. A medical aesthetician course near Vaughan typically introduces LED as part of a broader skin-treatment curriculum.

Set realistic expectations

LED supports results over a series rather than delivering a single dramatic change. Frame it that way with clients.
