Medical Aesthetician vs Esthetician: The Difference

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

The titles sound alike but describe different roles. A general esthetician typically works in spas and salons; a medical aesthetician works in clinical settings with more advanced skin protocols.

Scope and setting

Medical aestheticians often handle peels, dermaplaning, and clinical facials in a medical environment. Someone comparing a medical aesthetics course near North York with general esthetics is usually deciding how clinical they want their day-to-day work to be.
