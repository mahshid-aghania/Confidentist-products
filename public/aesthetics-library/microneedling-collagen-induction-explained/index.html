Microneedling (Collagen Induction Therapy), Explained

Microneedling & Collagen Induction5 min read

Microneedling — clinically, collagen induction therapy — uses fine needles to create controlled micro-channels that prompt the skin’s natural repair response. It is a popular skin-rejuvenation treatment.

Short but substantive

Certification is compact because it addresses one treatment well, covering device selection, depth protocols, safety, and hands-on practice. A microneedling course in Markham still includes supervised practice despite the short format.

Who it is for

It is designed for licensed estheticians, nurses, and comparable practitioners adding a service, and assumes basic anatomy knowledge.
