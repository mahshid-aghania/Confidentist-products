Prerequisites for Laser Training

Laser Technician Training5 min read

Reputable laser programs are not open to everyone off the street, and that is a good sign. Prerequisites usually include an existing esthetician or healthcare credential and current CPR certification.

Why the bar exists

A foundation in skin and client care lets the course focus on laser-specific skills. That is why a laser technician course for Vaughan residents assumes some prior background rather than teaching basic esthetics from scratch.

If you are starting fresh

If you do not yet meet the prerequisites, an esthetics or broader aesthetics program first is the usual route. Confirm the exact requirements with the provider.
