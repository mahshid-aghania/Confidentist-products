Professional Facials and Skin Treatments

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

A professional, clinically-oriented facial is more than relaxation — it starts with skin analysis and targets specific concerns with appropriate products and techniques.

Analysis first

Reading the skin and choosing the right approach is what makes a treatment effective. A medical aesthetics training for Scarborough residents program builds that analytical habit alongside hands-on technique.

Consistency and care

Results come from well-chosen, consistent treatment and good home-care advice. Training should cover both the treatment and the conversation around it.
