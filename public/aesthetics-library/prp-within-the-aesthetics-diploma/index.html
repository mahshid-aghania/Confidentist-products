Where PRP Fits Within the Aesthetics Diploma

Diplomas & Credentials5 min read

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy uses a client’s own processed blood to support skin and hair treatments. It is an advanced, clinically-sensitive procedure.

Part of a broader program

Rather than a standalone course, PRP training offered within the medical aesthetics diploma sits inside the injectable portion of the comprehensive program. Eligibility for those modules still depends on your professional scope.

Scope and rules first

Because PRP involves handling blood and may be a controlled act, confirm who may perform it with the relevant Ontario authorities before planning around it.
