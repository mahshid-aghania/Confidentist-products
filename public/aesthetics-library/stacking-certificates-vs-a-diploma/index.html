Stacking Certificates vs a Diploma

Diplomas & Credentials5 min read

"Certificate stacking" means adding one focused qualification at a time — lasers, then microneedling, then more — building a menu as you go. It is a flexible alternative to a diploma.

Pay-as-you-grow

Stacking spreads cost and lets you earn between courses, though it can take longer to reach the same breadth. Someone comparing stacking with a medical aesthetics diploma serving the GTA is really weighing flexibility against a single structured path.

Either way, plan the order

Sequence skills so each one supports the next, and confirm prerequisites for each course.
