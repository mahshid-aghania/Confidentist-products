The Laser Consultation, Explained

Laser Technician Training5 min read

The consultation is where safe, successful treatment is set up. It covers medical history, skin typing, contraindications, realistic expectations, and informed consent before any device is used.

Why it is a skill

A rushed consultation leads to poor outcomes and unhappy clients. Strong programs — including those profiled among the laser technician courses serving Toronto — teach consultation as a structured, teachable process.

Consent is ongoing

Good practitioners keep checking in throughout a series, not just at the first visit. Training should model that.
