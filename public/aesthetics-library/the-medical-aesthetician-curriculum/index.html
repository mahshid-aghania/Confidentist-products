Inside a Medical Aesthetician Curriculum

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

A medical aesthetician program generally pairs skin science — anatomy, physiology, product chemistry — with hands-on treatment training and some business basics.

Theory plus practice

Expect modules on facials, peels, dermaplaning, microdermabrasion, and LED, then supervised clinical practice. A credible medical aesthetics course serving Mississauga runs over several weeks precisely because both halves take time.

Check the scope

Confirm what is and is not included — this path is skin-focused and does not cover injectables or laser, which are separate programs.
