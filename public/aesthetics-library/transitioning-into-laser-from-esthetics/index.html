Transitioning Into Laser Work From Esthetics

Laser Technician Training5 min read

Moving from general esthetics into laser work is one of the most common career upgrades in the field. Your client-care and skin skills transfer; what you add is device-specific knowledge and safety.

A staged move

Treat it as a deliberate step: train, practise under supervision, then build a caseload. Those researching laser technician courses near Thornhill are usually estheticians planning exactly this kind of transition.

Keep your foundation

Your esthetics background remains an asset — it is what lets you assess skin well. Build on it rather than leaving it behind.
