What a Medical Aesthetician Actually Does

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

A medical aesthetician works across a broad menu of skin treatments in clinical settings — professional facials, chemical peels, dermaplaning, microdermabrasion, and LED therapy among them.

Clinical, not spa-only

The "medical" part means working in med spas, dermatology, and plastic-surgery clinics alongside clinicians. People exploring a medical aesthetician course for Toronto residents are usually drawn to this wider, clinically-oriented scope.

Know the boundaries

This role is skin-focused; it does not include injectables, and laser is a separate certification. Understanding scope helps you plan training.
