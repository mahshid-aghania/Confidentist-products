Who Can Inject Botox and Filler in Ontario?

Injectables (Medical Professionals)5 min read

Injectables are among the most tightly restricted treatments in aesthetics. In Ontario, injecting neurotoxins and fillers is tied to professional scope, so training is limited to qualified healthcare providers.

Eligibility comes first

Programs such as those explaining a Botox course for North York practitioners restrict enrolment to nurses, physicians, and comparable providers — or those with appropriate medical-director authorization. If you are not a healthcare professional, this is not an entry-level starting point.

Confirm the rules

Scope and requirements are set by regulatory colleges and can change. Verify your position before planning injectable training.
