Who Can Perform Laser Hair Removal in Ontario?

Laser Technician Training5 min read

Prospective students often ask whether laser hair removal is a regulated act in Ontario and who is allowed to perform it. The rules focus on training, safety, and the setting rather than a single provincial licence for "laser technician."

Training supports safe practice

Because improper use can harm skin, documented training and safety competence matter. Those weighing a laser hair removal course for Toronto residents should prioritise programs with real supervised practice and clear safety modules.

Confirm the current rules

Regulations and clinic requirements change, so verify the current position with the relevant Ontario authorities and your employer. A completion certificate demonstrates training; it is not a licence in itself.
