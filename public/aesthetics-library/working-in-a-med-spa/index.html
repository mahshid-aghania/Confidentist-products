Working in a Med Spa: What to Expect

Medical Aesthetician & Skin5 min read

Med spas blend a clinical standard of care with a client-experience focus. Working in one means delivering skin treatments competently while making clients feel looked after.

A clinical environment

Expect documentation, hygiene standards, and collaboration with clinicians. People pursuing medical aesthetics training serving the GTA are often aiming squarely at med-spa roles, so training that mirrors that environment helps.

Professionalism counts

Reliability, communication, and clean technique are what employers notice. The certificate gets you in the door; conduct keeps you there.
