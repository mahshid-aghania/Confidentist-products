Subject-Based

AFK Subject-Based Program

Prefer a focused, flexible option? Select individual AFK subjects and concentrate your time and budget on the areas where you need the most support. Tap a subject below to see what it covers.

01Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery
Principles of exodontia, surgical techniques, and the recognition and management of common surgical complications.
02Local Anesthesia
Anesthetic agents, injection techniques and relevant anatomy, plus recognition and management of anesthetic complications.
03Oral Pathology
Diseases and lesions of the oral and maxillofacial region and their clinical and histological features.
04Pharmacology
Drug classes relevant to dentistry, their mechanisms, interactions and prescribing considerations.
05Periodontics
Periodontal anatomy, disease classification, diagnosis, and non-surgical and surgical therapy.
06Oral Radiology
Imaging principles, radiographic techniques and interpretation of common radiographic findings.
07Endodontics
Pulpal and periapical diagnosis, root canal anatomy and the principles of endodontic treatment.
08Prosthodontics
Fixed and removable prosthodontics, occlusion and treatment planning for tooth replacement.
09Restorative Dentistry & Dental Materials
Operative procedures and the properties of the dental materials used in restorative treatment.
10Orthodontics
Growth and development, malocclusion, diagnosis and the principles of orthodontic treatment.
11Biomedical Sciences
Foundational anatomy, physiology, microbiology and biochemistry underpinning clinical dentistry.
12Pediatric Dentistry
Growth, behaviour management and treatment considerations for child and adolescent patients.
13Oral Medicine & Diagnosis
Clinical diagnosis, oral manifestations of systemic disease and the management of medically complex patients.
