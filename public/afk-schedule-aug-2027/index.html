AFK Extended — August 2027 Schedule

The complete session-by-session plan for the August 2027 AFK Extended cohort — the Extended head-start, the core study blocks, mini & full mock exams and final reviews before your NDEB AFK exam.

Course start: Nov 27, 2026 Program length: 8 months NDEB exam: August 2027
How the Program Runs

Your Path to the August 2027 Exam

The schedule opens with an Extended pre-course track from late November 2026, then moves into five structured study blocks covering the full AFK syllabus — each reinforced with quizzes, discussions and mock exams — finishing with reviews and full mock exams before your exam sitting.

Please be advised that the information below is subject to change. Session dates, times and formats may be adjusted; registered students are notified of any updates through ConfiPortal and the VIP WhatsApp group.
