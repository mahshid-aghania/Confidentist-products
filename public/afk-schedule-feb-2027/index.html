How the Program Runs

Your Path to the February 2027 Exam

The schedule opens with optional Extended sessions in June 2026 to get you started early, then moves into the core Comprehensive program — five structured study blocks covering the full AFK syllabus, each reinforced with quizzes, discussions and mock exams, finishing with final reviews ahead of your exam sitting.

Please be advised that the information below is subject to change. Session dates, times and formats may be adjusted; registered students are notified of any updates through ConfiPortal and the VIP WhatsApp group.
