Which bundles are available?
ConfiDentist offers course and mock-exam bundles that combine multiple programs, including an AFK + ACJ bundle and an all-inclusive NDECC package. Current bundle line-ups and availability are listed on the Course Bundles page.
How do I register for a bundle?
Choose your bundle on the Course Bundles page and use its Register button to complete enrollment.
What are the benefits of bundling?
Bundles are designed to provide comprehensive preparation, coordinated learning resources, and greater savings compared with enrolling in each program on its own.
