Start with the big picture

The Canadian dental equivalency journey usually moves through the AFK, ACJ and NDECC examinations, followed by Virtual OSCE practice. Select your current stage above, or explore these starting points:

  • AFK preparation course — the usual first equivalency exam for internationally trained dentists.
  • ACJ preparation course — build the clinical judgement the assessment evaluates.
  • NDECC Clinical Skills and Situational Judgement preparation — practical clinical training.
  • Virtual OSCE preparation — rehearse simulated OSCE stations online.
  • ADAT preparation and university dental interview preparation for admissions.
  • Compare course bundles or try a dental mock exam.
