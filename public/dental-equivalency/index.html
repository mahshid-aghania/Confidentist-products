Dental Equivalency · A General Educational Overview

The ConfiDentist Preparation Roadmap

Two distinct pathways ConfiDentist supports. This is a general overview for orientation — not every candidate follows exactly the same route, and steps may overlap.

Explore ConfiDentist Programs → Find My Program

Equivalency Examination Pathway Overview

Step 01

AFK

Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge — a written exam of the biomedical and dental sciences. ConfiDentist offers full-package and subject-based preparation.

AFK Preparation
Step 02

ACJ

Assessment of Clinical Judgement — a scenario-based written exam of clinical reasoning and decision-making, prepared for with the Comprehensive program.

ACJ Preparation
Step 03

NDECC

A performance assessment with Clinical Skills and Situational Judgement components. ConfiDentist offers focused tracks, mock programs and a complete bundle.

NDECC Preparation
Practice

Virtual OSCE®

Simulated OSCE stations delivered online so you can rehearse under realistic, exam-like conditions from anywhere in your preparation.

Virtual OSCE®
