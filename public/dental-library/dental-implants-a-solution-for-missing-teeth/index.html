Dental Implants: A Solution for Missing Teeth

Restorative Dentistry & Tooth Replacement6 min read

Losing a tooth can affect how you chew, speak, and feel about your smile. Dental implants are one option for replacing missing teeth, designed to look and function much like natural teeth. Learning how they work can help you have an informed conversation with your dentist about whether they suit your situation.

What a Dental Implant Is

An implant is a small post, usually made of titanium, that is placed into the jawbone to serve as a replacement tooth root. Over time, the bone grows around the post to hold it securely. A connector and a custom crown are then attached to complete the restoration. The result is a stable replacement that does not rely on neighboring teeth for support.

Potential Benefits

Many patients consider implants because of the way they integrate with everyday life. While outcomes depend on individual circumstances, commonly cited advantages include:

  • A restoration that feels and functions similar to a natural tooth
  • Support for adjacent teeth, which may shift when a gap is left open
  • Preservation of jawbone stimulation where a tooth root is missing
  • No need to alter healthy neighboring teeth, as with some bridges

Is an Implant Right for You?

Implants require adequate bone to support the post and healthy gums to surround it. Your dentist will review your medical history, take images of your jaw, and discuss any conditions that could affect healing. The process typically spans several months, allowing time for the implant to bond with the bone before the final crown is placed. You can explore your candidacy and treatment steps with the Smile Dental Arts Centre dental team during an evaluation.

Caring for an implant is much like caring for natural teeth: brushing, flossing, and regular checkups all help keep the surrounding tissue healthy. With good maintenance, implants can serve as a long-term option for many people.
