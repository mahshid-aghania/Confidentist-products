Gum Disease: Causes, Symptoms, and Treatment

Gum & Periodontal Health5 min read

Gum disease is a common condition that affects the tissues supporting your teeth. Because it often develops slowly and with few early signs, many people are unaware they have it until it progresses. The encouraging news is that, especially in its early stages, gum disease is often manageable with professional care and good home habits.

What Causes Gum Disease?

Gum disease begins when plaque, a sticky film of bacteria, builds up along and beneath the gumline. If it is not removed through regular brushing, flossing, and cleanings, it can irritate the gums and lead to inflammation. Several factors can increase the likelihood of gum concerns, including smoking, certain health conditions, and inconsistent oral care.

Recognizing the Symptoms

  • Red, swollen, or tender gums
  • Gums that bleed easily during brushing or flossing
  • Persistent bad breath or an unpleasant taste
  • Gums that appear to be pulling away from the teeth
  • Loose teeth or changes in how your teeth fit together

The earliest stage, often called gingivitis, is frequently reversible with attentive care. If you notice any of these signs, the professionals at Smile Dental Arts Centre can evaluate your gums and recommend a plan suited to your needs.

Treatment and Prevention

Treatment depends on how far the condition has progressed. In many cases, a thorough professional cleaning combined with improved daily habits can help your gums recover. More advanced situations may call for a deeper cleaning or additional periodontal care. Prevention remains the strongest ally: brushing twice daily, flossing, and keeping up with routine visits go a long way toward healthy gums.
