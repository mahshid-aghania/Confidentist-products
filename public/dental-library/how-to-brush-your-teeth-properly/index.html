How to Brush Your Teeth Properly

Oral Hygiene & Daily Care4 min read

Brushing your teeth may feel like second nature, but small adjustments to your technique can make a meaningful difference. Effective brushing removes plaque, freshens your breath, and helps protect both your teeth and gums. The good news is that a gentle, consistent routine is usually all it takes to keep your smile in good shape between visits.

A Simple, Effective Technique

Aim to brush twice a day for about two minutes each time. Hold your toothbrush at roughly a 45-degree angle to your gumline and use short, gentle strokes. There is no need to scrub hard, firm pressure can irritate your gums and wear down enamel over time. Be sure to reach the outer, inner, and chewing surfaces of every tooth.

  • Use a soft-bristled toothbrush that fits comfortably in your mouth
  • Choose a fluoride toothpaste to help strengthen enamel
  • Brush for two minutes, covering all surfaces evenly
  • Gently brush your tongue to help freshen your breath
  • Replace your toothbrush every three to four months, or sooner if the bristles fray

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many people brush too quickly or press too hard. Rushing can leave plaque behind, while heavy pressure may harm delicate gum tissue. It also helps to wait a short while after acidic foods or drinks before brushing, since enamel can be more vulnerable right afterward. If you are unsure whether your technique is working, the team at Smile Dental Arts Centre is happy to offer a quick demonstration during your next appointment.

Supporting Your Routine

Brushing works best as part of a complete routine that includes flossing and regular checkups. Together, these habits help reduce your risk of cavities and gum concerns while keeping your mouth feeling fresh. Small, steady efforts each day add up to lasting benefits for your smile.
