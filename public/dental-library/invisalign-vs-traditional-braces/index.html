Invisalign vs. Traditional Braces

Orthodontics5 min read

Straightening your teeth can improve both the look of your smile and the health of your mouth, making it easier to clean between teeth and reducing uneven wear. Two of the most popular ways to achieve this are clear aligners, such as Invisalign, and traditional braces. Both can produce excellent results, and choosing between them comes down to your specific needs, lifestyle, and the complexity of your case.

How Each Option Works

Traditional braces use metal or ceramic brackets bonded to your teeth, connected by wires that are gradually adjusted to guide teeth into position. Clear aligners are a series of custom-made, removable trays that shift your teeth in small increments. You switch to a new set of aligners every week or two as your treatment progresses. The orthodontic team at Smile Dental Arts Centre can help determine which approach is best suited to your goals.

Comparing the Two

  • Appearance: Aligners are nearly invisible; braces are more noticeable
  • Removability: Aligners come out for eating and brushing; braces stay fixed
  • Complexity: Braces often handle more complex tooth movements effectively
  • Maintenance: Braces require careful cleaning around brackets; aligners are cleaned separately
  • Discipline: Aligners work best when worn 20 to 22 hours a day as directed

Which Is Right for You?

Clear aligners appeal to many adults and teens who value a discreet, flexible option and are comfortable keeping track of removable trays. Traditional braces remain a dependable choice, particularly for cases involving significant crowding, bite correction, or rotation that benefits from the precise control brackets provide. Your treatment time with either option depends on your individual situation.

The Importance of Professional Guidance

Whichever method you choose, orthodontic treatment should always be supervised by a qualified dental professional. A proper assessment, including records and imaging, ensures your teeth move safely and that your bite functions well once treatment is complete. Regular check-ins allow your provider to monitor progress and make adjustments as needed.
