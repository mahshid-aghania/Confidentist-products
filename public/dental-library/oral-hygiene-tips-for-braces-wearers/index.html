Oral Hygiene Tips for Braces Wearers

Orthodontics5 min read

Braces are a wonderful investment in a healthier, straighter smile, but the brackets and wires create plenty of new nooks where food and plaque can hide. With a few adjustments to your daily routine, you can keep your teeth and gums healthy throughout treatment and reveal a bright smile when the braces come off. A little extra effort each day goes a long way.

Brushing Around Brackets

Plan to brush after every meal when possible, using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Angle the brush toward the gumline, then clean above and below each bracket so plaque does not build up around the hardware. Take your time and work section by section. If you are ever unsure whether you are reaching every spot, the hygienists at our dental practice can demonstrate the right technique during your visit.

Cleaning Between Teeth

Flossing with braces takes a little patience, but it remains essential for healthy gums. Helpful tools include:

  • Floss threaders or orthodontic floss to slip beneath the wire.
  • Interdental brushes that fit between brackets and under wires.
  • A water flosser to rinse away debris in hard-to-reach areas.
  • A small mirror to check your work after cleaning.

Foods and Habits to Watch

Sticky, hard, or chewy foods can bend wires or loosen brackets, so it helps to cut crunchy items into smaller pieces and avoid things like caramel and hard candy. Limiting sugary drinks also reduces the risk of white spots forming around brackets. Keep a small travel kit with a toothbrush and floss for cleaning up after lunch on busy days.

Staying on Track

Regular dental checkups alongside your orthodontic appointments help catch any early concerns, such as inflamed gums or decalcification. Consistency is what protects your smile, and your dental team is there to support you at every stage.
