Pregnancy and Oral Health

Oral Health & Overall Wellness5 min read

Pregnancy brings many changes to the body, and your mouth is no exception. Hormonal shifts during these months can affect your gums and teeth in ways that may surprise you. The good news is that with a little extra attention and regular dental care, you can keep your smile healthy for yourself and support a positive start for your baby. Caring for your oral health is an important part of caring for your overall well-being during pregnancy.

How Pregnancy Can Affect Your Mouth

Higher hormone levels can make gums more sensitive and prone to swelling, tenderness, or bleeding, a condition often called pregnancy gingivitis. Some people also experience increased cravings for sugary foods or deal with morning sickness, both of which can affect the teeth. Being aware of these changes helps you respond to them thoughtfully rather than worrying about them.

Simple Steps to Protect Your Smile

A few gentle habits can go a long way during pregnancy:

  • Brush twice daily with a soft toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss once a day to keep gums healthy and reduce inflammation
  • Rinse with water after morning sickness, then brush a little later to protect enamel
  • Choose nutritious snacks and limit sugary foods and drinks
  • Stay hydrated to help maintain a comfortable, healthy mouth

Keeping up with dental visits is also encouraged. The caring professionals at Smile Dental Arts Centre can provide gentle cleanings and advice tailored to this special time.

Visiting the Dentist While Expecting

Routine dental care is generally considered safe during pregnancy, and preventive checkups can help manage gum changes before they progress. Always let your dental team know that you are pregnant and share any updates from your physician so your care can be coordinated. Many patients find that addressing concerns during pregnancy is more comfortable than postponing them, and your dentist can help you plan the timing of any treatment.
