Tooth Sensitivity: Causes and Remedies

Common Concerns & Symptoms5 min read

That sudden twinge when you sip something cold or bite into a sweet treat is a familiar experience for many people. Tooth sensitivity is common, and while it can be uncomfortable, it is often manageable once you understand what is causing it. Paying attention to these signals can also help you address underlying issues early.

What Causes Sensitive Teeth

Tooth sensitivity usually happens when the protective enamel wears thin or the gums recede, exposing the softer layer underneath called dentin. Dentin contains tiny channels that lead to the nerve, so hot, cold, sweet, or acidic triggers can cause a sharp, temporary sensation. Several factors can contribute to this:

  • Brushing too hard or using a stiff-bristled toothbrush
  • Gum recession that exposes the tooth root
  • Frequent consumption of acidic foods and drinks
  • Tooth grinding, which can wear down enamel over time
  • Cavities, worn fillings, or a cracked tooth

Remedies and Everyday Care

The right approach depends on the cause, which is why a dental assessment is so helpful. In the meantime, several steps can ease discomfort. Switching to a soft-bristled brush and using gentle, circular motions protects your enamel and gums. Desensitizing toothpastes, used consistently, can help calm the nerve response over several weeks. Cutting back on acidic foods and drinks, and rinsing with water afterward, also gives your enamel a chance to stay strong.

If you grind your teeth, a custom night guard may protect them while you sleep. For sensitivity caused by cavities or worn fillings, professional treatment addresses the root of the problem. The friendly team at this dental practice can help pinpoint the cause and recommend the most suitable options for you.

When to See Your Dentist

Occasional mild sensitivity is common, but sensitivity that is persistent, severe, or focused on one tooth deserves a closer look. It may signal a cavity, a crack, or a gum concern that benefits from timely care. A simple exam can bring both answers and relief, so you can get back to enjoying your favourite foods comfortably.
