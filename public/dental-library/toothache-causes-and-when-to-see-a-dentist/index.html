Toothache: Causes and When to See a Dentist

Emergency & Urgent Care5 min read

A toothache can range from a mild, passing sensitivity to a persistent, throbbing pain that makes it hard to eat, sleep, or concentrate. Because the discomfort often signals something happening beneath the surface, understanding the possible causes can help you decide how urgently to seek care. While some aches settle on their own, others point to problems that benefit from timely attention.

Common Causes of Tooth Pain

Toothaches arise for many reasons, and the underlying issue is not always obvious from the symptoms alone. A professional examination, sometimes with X-rays, helps identify the source and guide appropriate treatment.

  • Tooth decay that has reached the sensitive inner layers of the tooth
  • A cracked, chipped, or fractured tooth
  • Gum inflammation or infection around the tooth
  • An abscess, which is a pocket of infection near the root
  • Teeth grinding or clenching, often during sleep
  • Sinus pressure, which can mimic pain in the upper teeth

Easing Discomfort at Home

While you arrange to see a dentist, a few measures may offer temporary relief. Rinsing gently with warm salt water can soothe irritated tissue, and a cold compress against the cheek may reduce swelling. Over-the-counter pain relievers used as directed can help manage discomfort. These steps address symptoms rather than the cause, so they are best thought of as a bridge until you can be evaluated. If you are unsure how to manage ongoing pain, the team at Smile Dental Arts Centre can offer guidance and arrange an appointment.

When to See a Dentist Promptly

Certain signs suggest that waiting is not advisable. Seek care soon if your pain lasts more than a day or two, is severe, or returns repeatedly. Swelling of the face or jaw, fever, a bad taste, or pain when biting may indicate infection that needs professional treatment. Pain accompanied by difficulty breathing or swallowing warrants urgent medical attention.

Addressing a toothache early often means simpler treatment and a more comfortable recovery. Even if the ache eases, the underlying issue may remain, so a check-up is worthwhile once pain appears.

If a toothache is interfering with your day, schedule a visit with Smile Dental Arts Centre to find the cause and discuss your options.
