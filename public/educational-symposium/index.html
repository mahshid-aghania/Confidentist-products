About the symposium

Join us for a one-day Educational Symposium designed for dental professionals who want to strengthen their clinical decision-making through real cases — not just lectures.

Built on a problem-based, case-based learning approach, this symposium presents participants with real clinical problems first. Working through each case, participants identify what they know, what they need to learn, and how to apply current evidence to diagnosis, risk assessment, and treatment planning. The result is practical, clinically relevant learning that transfers directly to everyday practice.

Learning objectives

Upon completion of this symposium, participants will be able to:

  1. Analyze complex clinical cases presented in a problem-based format and formulate evidence-based diagnoses and treatment plans.
  2. Identify the key clinical information, risk factors, and diagnostic tests required to work up a case in each topic area.
  3. Compare treatment options for each case, weighing benefits, risks, limitations, and patient-specific factors.
  4. Recognize common complications and pitfalls, and apply strategies to prevent, identify, and manage them.
  5. Apply current standards and best practices from each topic area directly to everyday clinical practice.

Key topics

IPAC (148) Sedation (340) Perio (490) Prostho (610–670) Patho (010) Surgery (310) Endo (070)
