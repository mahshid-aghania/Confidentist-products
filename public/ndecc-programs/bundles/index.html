FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Brilliant Bundle include?
The bundle combines the Clinical Skills course and its mock exams with the Situational Judgement course and its mock exams, plus the basic tools you need to begin. It's designed as a complete pathway through both NDECC components.
Can I buy the components separately instead?
Yes. You can register for Clinical Skills or Situational Judgement individually, with mock exams available as add-ons. The bundle brings everything together in one package.
Are payment plans available?
Yes — the bundle can be paid in installments with zero interest. For details, contact info@confidentist.ca or call +1 437 370 0122.
Are the programs in person?
Both components include significant in-person work at the ConfiDentist centre — supervised lab access for Clinical Skills and small-group classes for Situational Judgement — alongside recorded content accessed through ConfiPortal.
Who can I contact with questions?
Reach out to info@confidentist.ca or call +1 437 370 0122. For technical support, contact admin@confidentist.ca or confiportal@confidentist.ca.
