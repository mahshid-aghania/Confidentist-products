FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Clinical Skills course in person?
It combines recorded technique demonstrations with 6 days of supervised, in-person lab access at the ConfiDentist centre. Recorded content is accessed through your ConfiPortal account.
What do I need to bring?
The program and mock exams are run in a fully equipped clinic setting with a complete set of instruments provided. For the mock exam, please bring your own loupes.
When will I receive access?
Access is generally provided within 2–3 business days after registration, along with your ConfiPortal details and scheduling information.
Are the mock exams part of the Core Package?
The Skills Mock Exam is a separate program you can add for exam-realistic rehearsal. If you'd like both the course and the mocks together with the Situational Judgement component, see the Brilliant Bundle.
Who can I contact with questions?
Reach out to info@confidentist.ca or call +1 437 370 0122. For technical support, contact admin@confidentist.ca or confiportal@confidentist.ca.
