FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How many mock exams can I take?
You can book 1 to 5 full mock exams. Pricing runs from $1,250 to $4,500 + HST depending on how many you choose.
Where are the mock exams held, and what do I bring?
Each mock runs in a fully equipped clinic with a complete set of instruments provided, 8 hours of supervision and full infection-control protocols — exactly as on exam day. Please bring your own loupes.
Do I get feedback?
Yes — each mock is followed by a dedicated 1-hour discussion with an instructor the next day to review your performance and your next steps.
Is this part of the Clinical Skills course?
The mock exam is a standalone program. For the full course plus mocks and the Situational Judgement component together, see NDECC Bundles — Your Complete Path to Success, or the NDECC Clinical Skills program.
Who can I contact with questions?
Reach out to info@confidentist.ca or call +1 437 370 0122. For technical support, contact admin@confidentist.ca.
