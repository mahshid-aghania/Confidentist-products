Orthodontics for GPs

Master the fundamentals of orthodontic diagnosis, treatment planning, biomechanics, and appliance systems — fixed or clear aligners.

About the course

This education program addresses practicing dentists interested in increasing their knowledge, improving patient experience, and increasing revenue by adding Orthodontics to their practice. This master’s course consists of lectures and hands-on sessions. The course works on philosophy, diagnostic skills, and practical knowledge to increase treatment success and case acceptance.

Course objectives

  1. Identify ideal cases to start incorporating orthodontic cases with fixed systems or clear aligner treatment in your practice, including orthodontic letters and forms.
  2. Compile the required diagnostic records — digital photography, treatment chart, study models, digital scanning, cephalometric, panorex, and periodontal exam.
  3. Understand basic biomechanics and its implications in orthodontics.
  4. Diagnose and treatment-plan: extraction or non-extraction, growth modification, early treatments, surgery or camouflage, and more.
  5. Growth and development, Phase I & removable expansion appliances.
  6. Fixed brackets and readjusted appliances.
  7. Different archwires, elastics, power chains, open coils, separators, and banding of molars.
  8. Retention, with real cases — before and after.
