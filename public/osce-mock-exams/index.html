FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this OSCE mock exam designed for?
The OSCE Mock Exam is designed for internationally trained dentists preparing for the NDEB Virtual OSCE — a key step toward Canadian dental licensure. It is ideal for candidates sitting the March 2027 exam.
How much is the OSCE mock exam?
One OSCE mock is $500, two mocks are $900 and three mocks are $1,300 (CAD), plus 13% HST, which is added automatically at checkout. Previous students are welcome to contact the office for a discount coupon code, which can be applied securely during checkout.
What is included?
You get one, two or three full simulated OSCE mocks under realistic exam conditions, a live instructor-led review of every station, personalized performance feedback, and a live Q&A — all delivered online.
When does the mock exam take place?
The mock is scheduled for March 2027, ahead of the NDEB OSCE. The exact session date, access link and preparation details are emailed upon enrollment, at least one week in advance.
What happens if I miss the discussion session?
No recordings are provided for missed live discussion sessions. It is essential to attend in real time (EST — Toronto time zone). If you missed a discussion, you must book an appointment to see the recorded discussion.
How do I enroll and pay?
Click any Enroll button to check out securely through Stripe. HST is applied automatically. You will receive a confirmation email with all session details.
