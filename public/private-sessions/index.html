How to Request a Private Session

To request a session, email admin@confidentist.ca with the details below. Our team coordinates the rest.

What to include in your email

  • Subject area — which subject you need help with (e.g., Radiology).
  • Instructor preference — if you have a specific instructor in mind.
  • Timeframe — your available hours (e.g., between 10 AM and 3 PM).
  • Preferred slot — the exact time you'd like within that range.
  • Brief explanation — a short note on your challenge (e.g., "I struggle with X-ray interpretation").

What happens next

  • Coordination — our admin team matches you with an available instructor.
  • Confirmation — we contact you to confirm your session details.
  • Payment — you complete your payment after confirmation.
  • Zoom link — you receive your session link in advance.
  • Session — meet with your instructor and focus on what matters most.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Private and Semi-Private Sessions?
They are tailored tutoring sessions that give you individualized attention on specific topics. You can book a one-on-one session or a semi-private session to dive deep into your topic of choice with expert guidance.
How do I book a session?
Email admin@confidentist.ca with your subject area, instructor preference, available timeframe, preferred slot, and a brief explanation of your challenge. Our admin team will coordinate the rest.
Can I choose my instructor?
Yes. Include your instructor preference in your request, and our team will match you with an available instructor who meets your needs.
When do I pay?
You complete your payment after your session details are confirmed. You'll then receive your Zoom link in advance of the session.
How are sessions delivered?
Sessions are held online via Zoom. You'll receive your session link ahead of time so you're ready to begin.
Are payment plans available?
Yes. ConfiDentist offers various interest-free payment plans. For details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
