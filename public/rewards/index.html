🏆 ConfiDentist Rewards Program

We Value Your Effort. We Reward Your Growth.

At ConfiDentist, we believe that your dedication deserves recognition.

While your true reward is the progress you make toward your professional goals, we want to thank and motivate you with something more tangible — a performance-based discount on your next course.

This isn't just a gift. It's our way of encouraging serious, consistent learning and meaningful engagement throughout your equivalency journey.

Reward Tiers

The More You Grow, the More You Save

Your total score places you in one of four tiers — each with its own discount on your next course.

⭐ Note

The final group will not only receive the maximum available discount, but will also gain access to our elite professional circles.

With opportunities to collaborate with ConfiDentist as instructors, mentors, or consultants, you'll also enjoy additional exclusive benefits.

Application Process

How to Apply

Five simple steps to earn your performance-based discount.

01

Download the Student Points Form (Excel file).

Download the Points Form
02

Fill in your achievements and engagement according to the listed parameters.

03

Email the completed form to admin@confidentist.com.

04

Our team will review, verify, and calculate your total score.

05

You'll receive a confirmation with your special discount code to apply to your next purchase.
