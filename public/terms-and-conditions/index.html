  • Confidentist reserves the right to void registration, If a student registers for any courses using a coupon code that is not explicitly designated for them. Confidentist may, at its discretion, communicate with the student to address the misuse of coupon codes.

Course Repeat Policy (AFK & ACJ)

1. Course Repeats

  • AFK and ACJ courses may be taken a maximum of two (2) times per student.
  • One initial enrollment.
  • One repeat only if the student attended at least 70% of live lectures in the previous batch.

2. Mock Exam Policy

  • Each mock exam may be taken once only.
  • Mock exams cannot be repeated.
  • Mock exams may be postponed one (1) time only, subject to company scheduling.
  • No further postponements or repeats will be granted.

3. Mock Discussion Policy (AFK & ACJ)

  • Mock discussions (live or pre-recorded) are provided one time only.
  • No repeats and no access to recordings are permitted.
  • Attendance is required at the scheduled time set by the company.
  • Failure to attend will result in forfeiture of access.
