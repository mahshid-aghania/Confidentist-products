How It Works

Program Format

The program blends on-demand recorded sessions you can revisit at your own pace with a private, one-on-one coaching session tailored to your goals.

Structured recorded sessions walk you through the questions and strategies step by step.

A private 50-minute coaching session focuses on your individual strengths and gaps.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the program include?
The program includes structured recorded preparation sessions, coverage of technical and scientific questions, ethical and situational scenarios, professional communication strategies, confidence coaching, a university interview question booklet, a private 50-minute coaching session, customized feedback, mock-interview practice, and on-demand video access.
Is the coaching session one-on-one?
Yes. The program includes a private 50-minute coaching session focused on your individual goals, strengths, and the areas where you need the most support.
Can I watch the sessions on my own schedule?
Yes. The preparation sessions are pre-recorded, and on-demand video access lets you watch and revisit the material at your own pace.
Does the program cover ethical and situational questions?
Yes. Alongside technical and scientific questions, the program prepares you for ethical and situational scenarios that commonly come up in admissions interviews.
Are payment plans available?
Yes. ConfiDentist offers various interest-free payment plans. For details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
What should I do if I experience technical issues?
For technical support, contact confiportal@confidentist.ca or admin@confidentist.ca. You can also call +1 437 370 0122.
