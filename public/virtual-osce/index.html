FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the course online?
Yes. The Virtual OSCE course is delivered entirely online, including both live and pre-recorded sessions. Live lectures are conducted via Zoom, and pre-recorded lectures can be viewed in your ConfiPortal account. Occasional in-person sessions may be available at the Toronto office.
Is mentorship included?
Yes. You will have a dedicated mentor available to answer your questions throughout the course.
Are payment plans available?
Yes. ConfiDentist offers various interest-free payment plans. For details, contact info@confidentist.ca.
Is live attendance mandatory?
Participation in the live sessions is highly recommended, but it is not mandatory — pre-recorded lectures and discussions are available on demand through ConfiPortal.
Are booklets delivered outside Canada?
Yes. Booklets are shipped internationally. Please note that shipping outside Canada may take 4–6 weeks.
When will I receive course access?
Access to the lectures is generally provided within 2–3 business days after registration, along with your ConfiPortal details.
Is there a student discount?
A university student discount may be available. Email admin@confidentist.ca from your university email address to ask about the current offer.
What should I do if I experience technical issues?
For technical support, contact confiportal@confidentist.ca or admin@confidentist.ca. You can also call +1 437 370 0122.
